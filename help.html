How Can We Help You?

Find instant answers to questions regarding our donation processing, tax exemption receipts, NGO verification audits, and volunteer onboarding.

1. Donation & Financial Questions

Yes. All associated welfare organizations on Give-AID hold valid tax-exempt status under Section 61 and Section 2(36) of the Income Tax Ordinance, 2001. Your computer-generated official receipt displays the organization's certified registration number for tax deduction filing.

The minimum donation on Give-AID is Rs. 100 in PKR. There is no upper limit for custom contributions. For institutional endowments exceeding Rs. 5,000,000, please contact our directorate directly for dedicated escrow arrangements.

Yes. When filling out the donation checkout form, simply check the "Make my donation anonymous" box. Your personal identity will not appear on public contribution registers or impact walls.

Zero percent is deducted from your contribution. Give-AID's server costs and administrative overhead are underwritten by our corporate CSR foundation partners, ensuring 100% of your money reaches field operations.

2. NGO Vetting & Field Audits

Every applicant must provide 3 consecutive years of audited balance sheets, valid Charity Commission registration certificates, proof of physical clinic/school infrastructure, and clean background checks for all trustees.

Submit an inquiry via our Contact page under the subject "NGO Partnership Application". Our compliance team will schedule an initial documentation review within 5 business days.

3. Volunteering & Event Participation
