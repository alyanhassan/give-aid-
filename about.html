Who We Are

Connecting Compassion with Verifiable Action.

Give-AID is a centralized humanitarian bridge built to unite multiple non-governmental organizations (NGOs), compassionate benefactors, volunteer forces, and underserved families under one transparent umbrella.

Our Genesis

Why Give-AID Was Born

In Pakistan, an estimated Rs. 650 billion is donated annually through philanthropic giving. Yet millions of deserving children remain out of school, underprivileged patients struggle to afford emergency dialysis, and remote villages lack clean drinking water.

The challenge was never a shortage of generosity — it was fragmentation and lack of unified transparency. Small, highly effective grassroots welfare trusts operated without digital discovery, while willing donors hesitated because they couldn’t trace the exact outcome of their contributions.

Give-AID solves this by vetting NGOs, standardizing cause reporting, and giving every donor an instant verifiable receipt and progress audit.

Guiding Principles

The Give-AID Standard

We operate under strict standards of fiduciary governance, digital verification, and emotional dignity for beneficiaries.

01.

Vetted NGO Certification

Every partner NGO undergoes rigorous on-ground verification, tax compliance audits, and background reviews before receiving platform disbursements.

02.

Traceable Dollar-to-Field

Every rupee contributed is assigned an immutable transaction code. Donors receive photographic confirmations and milestone updates.

03.

Uncompromising Dignity

We treat every recipient of healthcare, food, or education as an equal human being. No exploitative imagery; only authentic progress and hope.
