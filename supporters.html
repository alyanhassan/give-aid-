Our Partners in Humanitarian Action

Give-AID operates in tight collaboration with established welfare trusts, accredited clinical networks, and corporate foundations committed to transparent social development.

Grassroots Welfare Execution Partners

Edhi Foundation

VERIFIED NGO

Collaborating on national emergency ambulance fuel subsidies, destitute burial funds, and emergency infant adoption cradles across all 4 provinces.

Focus: Emergency Services & Shelter

The Citizens Foundation (TCF)

VERIFIED NGO

Co-funding female educator training, science labs, and textbook kits for over 1,900 purpose-built schools in low-income neighbourhoods.

Focus: Slum Primary & Secondary Education

Indus Hospital & Health Network

VERIFIED NGO

Direct partner for free tertiary surgical care, dialysis subsidies, and mobile diagnostic clinics reaching rural communities without public hospitals.

Focus: Paperless Free Healthcare

LRBT Eye Care Network

VERIFIED NGO

Eliminating curable blindness through free corneal grafts, cataract intraocular lenses, and school eye screening programs across 19 base hospitals.

Focus: Vision Restoration & Ophthalmology

Corporate CSR Allies

These organizations fund our technological platform infrastructure and cover third-party processing costs so 100% of public donations go straight to beneficiaries.
